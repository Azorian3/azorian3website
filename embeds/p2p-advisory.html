Zero-Touch AP reference architecture

Exceptions should not wait for a human to notice them. This pattern re-evaluates every blocked invoice on upstream events and alerts buyers before the invoice arrives.

01 CAPTURE
OCR / IDP intake

Invoice captured via VIM/IDP, header and lines extracted, vendor normalized against golden record.

02 MATCH
3-way + semantic match

Deterministic match on PO/GR; AI semantic matching for services and contractor entries.

03 PRE-EMPT
Budget & amendment alerts

PO budget and amendment alerts fire to the buyer before the invoice lands, not after it blocks.

04 SELF-HEAL
Event-driven re-evaluation

Blocked invoices re-evaluated on GR posting and PO change events; most clear with no touch.

05 GOVERN
Human-in-the-loop

True exceptions routed with SoD-aware approvals, full audit trail and recertification.
