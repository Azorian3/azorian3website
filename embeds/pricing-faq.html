Questions people ask about spend reports and vendor classification

Short answers from fifteen years of building procurement reporting across SAP, Oracle and dozens of ERPs.
Ask anything in the chat
What should a spend report include?

Total spend by category, supplier and business unit; PO versus non-PO and maverick spend; purchase price variance; payment terms and pay-on-time; supplier concentration and single-source exposure; and a savings pipeline from identified to realized. A good spend report also carries a forward view: a two-quarter spend forecast and a cash forecast so it drives decisions instead of describing last quarter.

How do you classify vendors and spend?

Every vendor and line item is mapped into one governed taxonomy, usually UNSPSC or a company-specific category tree, using AI classification agents trained on your invoice text, GL accounts and material groups. Duplicate vendors are merged, miscoded spend is reclassified, and every change is held for a human to approve so the taxonomy stays auditable.

What is spend under management and why does it matter?

Spend under management is the share of total spend that procurement actively sources, contracts or controls. World-class organizations run above 85 percent. Every point below that is spend where nobody negotiated the price, checked the supplier or captured the terms, which is why it is the first KPI on the Azorian report.

What is the difference between a spend report and a procurement report?

A spend report shows where the money went. A procurement report adds how it got there: PO compliance, cycle times, contract coverage, first-time match and touchless invoice rates, supplier risk and savings realization. Azorian combines both so procurement, finance and operations argue about the same numbers.

How is a cash forecast different from a spend forecast?

A spend forecast projects what will be committed and invoiced by category over the next two quarters. A cash forecast projects when money actually leaves the bank, by week, across supplier payables, payroll, tax, debt and CAPEX. Doing both from the same data is what lets a company plan liquidity and negotiate payment terms with confidence.

What is maverick spend and how do you reduce it?

Maverick spend is purchasing outside approved contracts, catalogs or purchase orders. The report isolates it by category, requester and supplier, then the fixes are process design: PO-first policy, guided buying, catalog coverage and contract compliance tracking, with AI agents flagging new off-contract vendors as they appear.

How fast can you deliver a first spend report?

Ten business days from receiving an ERP extract for a single instance. The AI agents do classification and cleansing in hours; the time goes into validating the taxonomy with your team and building the executive readout. If the first report is not delivered on day ten, the sprint is free.
