The commercial side

The same engine, pointed at revenue.

Procurement was the proving ground. Pricing, quoting, margin and customer data are the same kind of chaos, and they hide more money. We read the sell side of the ERP the way we read the buy side: every gap priced, every gap owned.

01

Price and margin leakage

Discounts nobody approved, list prices that never moved with cost, deals below floor. We price every one by customer and SKU and hand sales the list, ranked by dollars.

02

Quote to cash

Quote cycle time, order errors, credit holds, days to invoice, days to pay. The KPI discipline we run on purchasing, applied to the order book, with the same weekly cadence.

03

Customer and product data

Duplicate customers, dead SKUs, miscoded territories. The agents that clean vendor master clean customer master too, and a person approves every change.

04

Cost to serve and forecast

Which customers, channels and products still make money once freight, returns and payment terms are counted. A margin forecast your CFO will sign.
